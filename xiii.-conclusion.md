# XIII. Conclusion


***

## English

Voidify introduces native privacy to Solana primarily through Nova: a flexible-amount, non-custodial privacy pool with encrypted rolling balances, balance top-ups, partial withdrawals, relayer submission, and on-chain zero-knowledge verification. Nova removes the fixed-denomination bottleneck, so users—including large depositors—do not need to wait for another identical amount before using the pool. Classic remains available as a standardized fixed-denomination alternative. Together, these systems make privacy more practical without giving up transparency, auditability, or user control.
